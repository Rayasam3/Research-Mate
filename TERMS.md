# Terms of Use

Research Mate is a personal/educational project demonstrating an
agentic AI literature research assistant. Please read before use.

## No Guarantees

- This is a demo/portfolio project, not a commercial product.
- No uptime, availability, or accuracy guarantees are made.
- The service may be slow, rate-limited, or temporarily unavailable,
  especially under free-tier API limits (Groq, ArXiv, Semantic Scholar).

## AI-Generated Content

- Summaries, gap analyses, and Related Work drafts are generated by
  large language models and may contain errors or inaccuracies.
- Always verify important claims against the original papers before
  relying on them academically or professionally.
- Citations are generated from paper metadata programmatically; verify
  formatting against your target style guide before formal submission.

## Data & Privacy

- Search topics and generated summaries may be temporarily cached
  (Redis, local disk) to improve performance and reduce redundant API
  calls. This data is not sold, shared, or used for any purpose beyond
  operating the service.
- No user accounts or personal data collection are implemented.
- Downloaded PDFs are sourced from publicly available academic
  repositories (ArXiv, OpenAlex, etc.) and are not redistributed.

## Fair Use

- This service depends on free-tier external APIs (ArXiv, Semantic
  Scholar, PubMed, OpenAlex, Groq). Please avoid excessive automated or
  scripted use that could degrade the service for others or violate
  those providers' own usage policies.
